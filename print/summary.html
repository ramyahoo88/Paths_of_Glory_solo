Bot action round — draw 1 Staff card, then take the first step that applies

  1. Forced play (Guns of August, CP turn 1 round 1).
  2. MO last chance: rounds 5–6, MO unmet, and the Attack Search has an MO attack → Operations with it.
  3. Urgent event: highest legal event of priority 8–9 (ties: lower OPS, lower number).
  4. MO: round 3+, MO unmet, and the Attack Search has an MO attack → Operations with it.
  5. Crisis: a bot VP/capital/fort space is empty with an enemy unit adjacent, and the Move list has a Fill → Operations.
  6. Rebuild (Allied bot only): no RP card played this turn and missing steps − RP held ≥ 6 → play a card for RP.
  7. Plan on the Staff card: Ops = Attack, Event 5+, Logistics · Event = Event 5+, Attack, Logistics · Logistics = Logistics, Attack, Event 5+.
  8. Fallback: Event 3–4 → moves-only Operations (a Capture, Fill or Siege, or 2+ moves) → RP/SR at low triggers → 1-OP Automatic Operation.

Attack Search

  1. Targets: enemy-occupied spaces and unbesieged enemy forts the bot can legally attack.
  2. Shortlist: the 5 targets with the largest CF difference (attack CF − defence CF − fort), plus Objectives and MO targets.
  3. Attackers: adjacent bot spaces, strongest first, while activation costs fit the OPS available.
  4. Edge = bot's expected hits − defender's (Edge table, after shifts). Score = Edge + bonuses (sheet 2).
  5. Highest Score first: attack if Score ≥ Stance (CP: Assault 0, Press +0.75, Hold +1.5; Allies +1 more). From round 5 an MO attack is made at any Score.
  6. Up to 3 attacks, each space used once; leftover OPS go to the Move list.

Card for Operations

  1. Plan costs 1 and ≤1 attack → Automatic Operation.
  2. Else the lowest-priority card with enough OPS for the attacks (or the first move); priority 6+ only if nothing else; ties: lower OPS, lower number. None enough: highest OPS, then replan.

Guard: leaving a bot space in contact, leave the weakest unit (except to capture a VP space). Never overstack, never end out of supply or in a fort space you cannot besiege.

Logistics

RP if missing steps ≥ 3 (army 2 eliminated / 1 reduced; corps 1 eliminated / ½ reduced): card covering most of them. SR if units worth ≥ 4 SR points sit in the Reserve Box or >3 spaces from the enemy: card whose SR value is closest. Cards of priority 7+ are never used for RP or SR.

Combat

  • Combat cards: attacking, play every usable one; defending, play them for VP, fort or trench spaces.
  • Flank attack: never attempt.
  • Losses: full corps, then reduced corps, then full armies, then reduced armies; lowest CF first.
  • Retreat: to a bot-controlled supplied space, preferring one not in contact and closest to a bot capital.
  • Hold ground: defending a VP space with a full-strength unit, take the extra step to cancel a retreat when the rules allow.
  • Advance: into the battle space if it is a VP, fort or Objective space, or with an Assault card; only into supplied spaces.

Turn phases

  • Replacements: reduced/eliminated armies in contact, other armies, then corps.
  • Reinforcements: legal arrival space nearest the current Staff card's front.
  • Draw: discard combat cards beyond two.
