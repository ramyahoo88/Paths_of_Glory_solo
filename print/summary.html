Bot action round — draw 1 Staff card, then take the first step that applies

  1. Forced play (Guns of August, CP turn 1 round 1).
  2. Urgent event: highest legal event of priority 8–9 whose condition holds (ties: lower OPS).
  3. Mandated Offensive: MO unmet, round 3+, and an MO attack exists → Operations with it.
  4. Crisis: a bot VP/capital/fort space is empty with an enemy unit adjacent → Operations with a Fill move; else SR a unit into it.
  5. Plan on the Staff card: Ops = Attack, Event 5+, Logistics · Event = Event 5+, Attack, Logistics · Logistics = Logistics, Attack, Event 5+.
  6. Fallback: Event 3–4 → moves-only Operations → RP/SR at low triggers → 1-OP Automatic Operation.

Attack Search

  1. Fronts: Staff card front, then West, East, Italy, Balkans, Near East.
  2. Targets on a front: Objective → MO targets (if MO unmet) → VP/fort spaces → others. Within a group: most bot CF able to attack, then Tie.
  3. Attackers: adjacent bot spaces, strongest first, while activation costs fit the OPS available.
  4. Edge = bot's expected hits − defender's (Edge table, after shifts).
  5. Attack the first target meeting the Stance (Assault 0, Press +0.75, Hold +1.5); Objective −1; MO easier (see table).
  6. Repeat with unused OPS and spaces, max 3 attacks; leftover OPS to moves.

Card for Operations

  1. Cost 1 → Automatic Operation.
  2. Else lowest event priority card with enough OPS (priority 6+ only if nothing else); ties: lower OPS, lower number.

Moves (in order)

  1. Fill a threatened space (empty bot VP/capital/fort, enemy within 2): nearest stack within 3 not in contact (or 2+ units).
  2. Capture an adjacent empty enemy VP space.
  3. Siege an adjacent empty enemy fort (army, or corps ≥ fort value).
  4. Capture any adjacent empty enemy space.
  5. Entrench an army in contact (Hold card: before Capture).
  6. Forward: rear stack toward nearest contact space with <3 units, nearest the Objective.

Guard: leaving a bot space in contact, leave the weakest unit (except to capture a VP space). Never overstack, never end out of supply or in a fort space you cannot besiege.

Logistics

RP if missing steps ≥ 3 (army 2 eliminated / 1 reduced; corps 1 eliminated / ½ reduced): card covering most of them. SR if units worth ≥ 4 SR points sit in the Reserve Box or >3 spaces from the enemy: card whose SR value is closest. Cards of priority 7+ are never used for RP/SR.

Combat

  • Combat cards: attacking, play every usable one; defending, play them for VP, fort or trench spaces.
  • Flank attack: never attempt.
  • Losses: full corps, then reduced corps, then full armies, then reduced armies; lowest CF first.
  • Retreat: to a bot-controlled supplied space, preferring one not in contact and closest to a bot capital.
  • Hold ground: defending a VP space with a full-strength unit, take the extra step to cancel a retreat when the rules allow.
  • Advance: into the battle space if it is a VP, fort or Objective space, or with an Assault card; only into supplied spaces.

Turn phases

  • Replacements: reduced/eliminated armies in contact, other armies, then corps.
  • Reinforcements: legal arrival space nearest the current Staff card's front.
  • Draw: discard combat cards beyond two.
