Bot action round — draw 1 Staff card, then take the first step that applies

Front order: card's front, then W, E, It, Bal, NE. CP bot: West first, then the card's front.

  1. Forced play (Guns of August, CP turn 1 round 1).
  2. MO last chance: rounds 5–6, MO unmet, the planned attacks include an MO attack → that Operation.
  3. Urgent event: highest legal event of priority 7–9 (ties: lower OPS, lower number).
  4. MO: round 3+, MO unmet, the planned attacks include an MO attack → that Operation.
  5. Crisis: a bot VP/capital/fort space is empty with an enemy unit adjacent, and the Operation planned with the highest OPS card includes any Fill → Operation with the cheapest card that pays for the first planned move (replan, attacks first).
  6. Rebuild (Allied bot only): no RP card yet this turn and missing steps − RP held ≥ 6 → RP (§7.1).
  7. Plan on the Staff card: Ops = Attack, Event 5+, Logistics · Event = Event 5+, Attack, Logistics · Logistics = Logistics, Attack, Event 5+.
  8. Fallback: Event 3–4 → moves-only Operation (a Capture, Fill or Siege, or 2+ moves) → RP/SR at low triggers → 1-OP Automatic Operation.

Attack Search

  1. Targets: enemy-occupied spaces and unbesieged enemy forts the bot can legally attack.
  2. Shortlist: the 3 largest CF differences (CF of all bot units able to attack − defending CF − fort), plus Objectives and MO targets.
  3. Edge = bot's expected hits − defender's (Edge table values as printed, after shifts; a 0-CF defender still fires). Score = Edge + bonuses (sheet 2). Rank by Score.
  4. From the top: take attacking spaces strongest first, skipping any the remaining OPS cannot pay or whose units cannot legally join (mixed nationalities from 2+ spaces need one space holding all of them); rescore (MO bonus only if the chosen units satisfy the MO); attack if Score ≥ Stance (CP: Assault +0.75, Press +1.5, Hold +2.25; Allies: +1, +1.75, +2.5). From round 5 an MO attack is made at any Score.
  5. Up to 3 attacks, each space once; leftover OPS go down the Move list.
  6. Resolving: recompute each Edge, keep the bonuses (drop the MO bonus if the MO is no longer met); skip if >0.5 below the Stance (never an attack that still meets the MO). Then unused attackers may hit the best Edge ≥ Stance.

Card for Operations

  1. Whole plan (attacks + moves) costs 1 and ≤1 attack → Automatic Operation.
  2. Else the lowest-OPS card that is enough for the attacks (or the first planned move), avoiding priority 6+; ties: lower priority, lower number. Combat cards count as priority 0. None enough: highest OPS, then replan.

Logistics

RP if missing steps − RP held ≥ 3 (fallback 1.5): the card covering most missing steps (at least 2; fallback 1). SR if units worth ≥ 4 SR points (fallback 1) sit in the Reserve Box or >3 spaces from the enemy: card whose SR value is closest (max 5); redeploy them: armies, then corps; Reserve Box first, then Tie. Never a priority 7+ card for RP or SR. Card ties: lower priority, lower number. No RP or SR right after the same play; no RP in the Influenza turn.

Combat and phases

  • Combat cards: attacking, play every usable one; defending, play them in VP, fort or trench spaces; always use already-played ones that apply. For OPS/SR/RP a combat card counts as priority 0.
  • Flank attack: never (Wireless Intercepts is played when allowed).
  • Losses (within the rules' loss allocation): full corps, reduced corps, full armies, reduced armies; lowest CF first.
  • Retreat: no overstack > bot-controlled > supplied > not in contact > nearest bot capital > most bot units > Tie.
  • Hold ground: in a VP space or capital with a full-strength unit, take the extra step to cancel a retreat where allowed.
  • Advance: into a VP, fort or own-front Objective-list space, or with an Assault or Press card; strongest first, up to 3 in the space; leave one unit in an emptied space still in contact; one space only.
  • Replacements: armies in contact, other armies on the map, eliminated armies, then corps likewise; ties: Tie. Rebuilt armies: earliest front, W, E, It, Bal, NE.
  • Event choices (§9): Landwehr in Replacement order; War in Africa: a corps (Reserve Box first); Salonika only with an eligible corps; neutrals next to the enemy; Kerensky, Brusilov: first Russian attack.
  • Reinforcements: no overstack > earliest front > nearest an enemy > Tie.
  • Draw: 3+ combat cards: discard lowest keep value to 2.
